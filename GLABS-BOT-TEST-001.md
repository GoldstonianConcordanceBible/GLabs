Will automated Pons participants interact with a newly launched token when the project has:

- GitHub
- Telegram
- no X account
- no Discord
- no paid promotion

## H1 — Onchain Discovery Hypothesis

Early automated Pons participation does not require X, Discord, or public social promotion because automated participants can detect new launches directly from onchain launch events and inspect the new market programmatically.

## H2 — Zero-Capital Replication Hypothesis

A legitimate educational/research launch with zero developer purchase may generate external trading activity and creator-fee accrual without creator inventory or creator-initiated trading.

## Wallet Classification

BOT-LIKELY:
Observable programmatic behavior such as extremely rapid launch response, repeated interactions across new Pons launches, repetitive sizing/timing, or automated round trips.

HUMAN-LIKELY:
Only when affirmative evidence supports the classification.

UNKNOWN:
Default classification when evidence is insufficient.

Do not label a wallet a bot merely because it traded early.

## Success Criteria

This experiment is NOT successful because price goes up.

Useful outcomes include:

A. bots arrive without social promotion;
B. no bots arrive;
C. activity occurs but wallets remain unclassifiable;
D. creator fees are generated;
E. no creator fees are generated.

Every outcome is evidence.

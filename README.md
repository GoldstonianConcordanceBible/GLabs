# What Is GLabs?

GLabs is an independent research and education laboratory exploring Web4 through real-world digital-market experiments.

Bitcoin is the first use case.

GLabs is designed to study the convergence of:

- Bitcoin
- blockchain infrastructure
- artificial intelligence
- autonomous agents
- digital markets
- provenance
- decentralized science
- machine payments
- education
- Web4

The laboratory asks:

> **What did we know before the outcome?**

---

# Use Case 001 — Bitcoin

Bitcoin provides the first GLabs research environment because it operates:

- globally
- continuously
- on public blockchain infrastructure
- with transparent market data
- with programmable settlement
- alongside rapidly developing AI and agent infrastructure

Bitcoin is the research subject.

GLabs is the laboratory.

Any future GLabs companion token is a separate experimental asset.

# Research Doctrine

Observation ≠ interpretation.

Interpretation ≠ verification.

Model agreement ≠ proof.

Prediction ≠ outcome.

Token price ≠ research quality.

A successful trade does not prove a thesis was rigorous.

A failed trade does not automatically invalidate useful research.
